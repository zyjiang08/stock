# Rebalance frequency comparison — 20261027T101313Z

Added three separate tables for 3-month, half-year and annual 9Sig signal
checks. Each table independently computes 40%, 50% and 60% cash allocations
with the same prices, date range, initial capital and rule settings. It shows
ending assets, cumulative return, CAGR, maximum drawdown and actual trade
count. Users can inspect check dates and export all nine strategies as CSV.
Input changes invalidate retained results and disable their export until the
next successful calculation.

## Calculation assumptions

- This extends the existing 9Sig signal model to natural quarter, half-year
  and year ends. A scheduled check may produce no trade. It does not reset
  the cash/ETF ratio unconditionally at every scheduled date.
- The default 9% quarterly ordinary growth target compounds to 18.81% per
  half-year and 41.158161% per year. The original engine remains unchanged.
- The 30Down lookback is converted to whole decision periods, rounded up:
  the default eight quarters become four half-years or two years. The
  drawdown threshold, ignored-sell count and 100% extreme-gain threshold
  retain their configured values. The latter measures gains over each
  decision interval. Rules run only on scheduled checks.
- All nine drawdowns use initial capital and the same quarter-end valuation
  grid, including quarters with no trade. Annual schedules therefore retain
  intermediate quarterly losses. These are not daily maximum drawdowns.
- Each calendar period uses its last available observation. The first period
  records holdings without a subsequent check. Partial periods and windows
  with no subsequent checks are disclosed alongside each table; CAGR is
  omitted for windows shorter than 365 days.
- Cash earns no interest; fractional shares are allowed; trading costs,
  slippage and taxes are excluded, as in the existing simulation.

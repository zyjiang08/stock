# Cash allocation comparison — 20261027T094244Z

Added an independent section comparing 40%, 50% and 60% cash strategies
(60%, 50% and 40% ETF respectively) with the same price data, date window,
capital and rule settings. Each uses its own initial/reset allocation and a
fresh original engine state. The engine itself remains unchanged.

The section displays cumulative return, CAGR, maximum quarterly drawdown,
ending assets and trade count, with three interactive return/drawdown curves,
a synchronized quarter slider and a separate comparison CSV export. Parameter
edits and failed requests mark retained results stale and disable export.
The single-strategy ETF slider does not change the three comparison allocations.
